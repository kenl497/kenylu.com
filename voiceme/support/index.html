Reporting a problem

It helps a lot if your email includes:

  • Your device (for example, iPad Air) and iPadOS or macOS version, from Settings, General, About
  • The VoiceMe version, from the App Store page or the app's settings
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can

Common questions

Does VoiceMe need an internet connection?

No. Typing, saved phrases, history and speech all work offline, so VoiceMe keeps working in the car, at the park or anywhere without Wi-Fi.

How do I get a clearer or more natural voice?

VoiceMe uses the voices installed on your iPad. You can download higher-quality voices for free:

  1. Open Settings, then Accessibility, then Spoken Content.
  2. Tap Voices, choose a language, and pick a voice marked Enhanced or Premium to download it.
  3. Choose that voice in VoiceMe's settings.
VoiceMe isn't making any sound.

Check that the volume is up and that the iPad isn't connected to Bluetooth headphones or a speaker you didn't expect. If speech still doesn't play, restart the iPad and try again, then email me if it keeps happening.

Where are my phrases and history stored?

Only on your device. VoiceMe has no accounts and doesn't send your words anywhere. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I move my phrases to a new iPad?

Your phrases come along when you set up the new iPad from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. VoiceMe doesn't sync between devices on its own.

Can I keep my child from leaving the app?

Yes. Use Guided Access, built into iPadOS: turn it on in Settings, then Accessibility, then Guided Access. Then triple-click the top or Home button while VoiceMe is open to lock the iPad to the app.

I'm a speech-language pathologist or teacher. Can I give feedback?

Please do. Feedback from people who work with AAC users every day shapes what gets built next. Email me anytime.
