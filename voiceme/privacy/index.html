VoiceMe privacy policy

VoiceMe doesn't collect your data. Everything you type, save and say stays on your device.

Who we are

VoiceMe is an augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) app made by Ken Lu, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use VoiceMe on iPad.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal information. VoiceMe does not send what you type or speak to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To work, VoiceMe saves some information locally, inside the app's storage on your device:

  • Words, phrases and sentences you type or save, including favorites and groups
  • Your speech history, so you can repeat things quickly
  • The audit log of what was spoken, for caregivers who use it
  • App settings, such as voice, speaking rate and history size
  • If you choose to sign in with Apple, your Apple sign-in identifier and the name and email Apple shares with the app

This information never leaves your device through VoiceMe. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, Apple may include VoiceMe's data in that backup, as it does for most apps. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Optional Sign in with Apple

You can use every part of VoiceMe without an account. If you choose to sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an identifier for your account and, the first time you sign in, the name and email you choose to share (you can hide your email using Apple's relay address). VoiceMe keeps these only on your device, to show that you're signed in. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

You can sign out or delete the account at any time in VoiceMe's Settings, which removes this information from the device. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for VoiceMe in your Apple Account settings.

Optional tips

VoiceMe is free. You can leave an optional tip in Settings; a tip unlocks nothing. Tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy. We never see or receive your payment details.

Speech

VoiceMe speaks using the voices built into iPadOS and macOS. Speech is generated on your device by Apple's system speech engine. The text being spoken is not sent to us.

What VoiceMe doesn't do

  • No analytics, crash reporting services or advertising
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier
  • No third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos
  • No sale or sharing of data, because we have none

Children

VoiceMe is designed to be used by children, including children under 13, often with help from a parent, teacher or therapist. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's rules for apps used by kids.

Keeping it private on a shared device

Anyone who can use the device can see what VoiceMe has saved, including history and the audit log. If the iPad is shared, consider a device passcode and Guided Access, both in the iPad's Settings.

Deleting your information

You can delete saved phrases and history inside the app. Deleting VoiceMe from your device removes all of its data from that device. Copies may remain in older device backups until those backups are deleted or replaced.

Changes to this policy

If VoiceMe ever changes how it handles information, for example by adding optional sync between your devices, we will update this page and the date at the top before that change ships. We will not start collecting information without saying so here first.
